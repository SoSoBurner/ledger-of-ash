# Mimolot Academy — Text RPG Flavor Packet

**Parent polity:** House Mimolot
**Type:** Academy — noble education, magical schooling, tariff-backed knowledge systems
**Truth layer:** direct_canon

## Atmosphere

The Academy's outer perimeter is a wall of pale granite quarried in a color that doesn't match anything else in the surrounding region — which is the point. House Mimolot commissioned it specifically to read as foreign, as a statement of independence from local stone and local aesthetic. Inside the wall the buildings are older and the stone is warmer, and the contrast between the exterior declaration and the interior use gives the campus a quality of having two natures: the face it shows the outside, and the working environment underneath.

Scholarly life runs on timed intervals. Bells mark the period changes, and students move between them with a purposefulness that reads less like urgency and more like the internalized rhythm of people who have been corrected for lateness enough times that punctuality has become instinctual. The magical infrastructure — warding lines set into the ground paths, observation towers with their shuttered apertures — is not hidden but it is not explained. It is present the way the walls are present. The assumption is that you understand what it does or that understanding it is not required for your business here.

## Scene Openers

1. **Dawn, the student courtyard.** A second-year student is testing a ward technique against a practice target — a chalk circle that the ward is supposed to seal without cracking the outer line. She has gotten it to ninety percent twice and the last ten is failing for a reason she has not identified. A third-year watches from the archway with an expression that suggests he knows the reason and is waiting to see whether she will find it herself.
2. **Midmorning, the examiner's corridor.** A visiting lecturer from an outside institution is waiting for his verification papers to clear before he can access the restricted archive. The clerk processing his paperwork is thorough in a way that signals institutional caution rather than personal suspicion. The lecturer understands the distinction and is being patient, because impatience in this corridor is noted on a form.
3. **Afternoon, the faculty common hall.** Two senior scholars are disagreeing about the attribution of a theoretical framework in the draft of a monograph. Their disagreement is entirely civil and entirely irresolvable, because one of them has a primary source the other considers methodologically compromised. They have been at this for three weeks.
4. **Evening, the outer ward gates.** A student is being escorted out by two security staff. The student is not struggling. The escort is not rough. The silence between all three parties is complete and specific in the way of a situation everyone present understands and none of them will describe later.

## Social Register

- Academic rank is addressed formally and without exception. First names are used only by explicit mutual invitation, and the invitation is not assumed by seniority alone. Getting this wrong in front of students marks the speaker as either from outside the academy tradition or deliberately provocative.
- The archive access system is tiered and the tiers are enforced by institutional process, not individual discretion. Asking a librarian to grant access above your current clearance puts them in a position they are not authorized to fill. The correct channel is a written request to the archive committee.
- Magical practice areas are marked with specific signage that indicates active-ward zones, observation zones, and prohibited zones. Entering a marked area without reading the sign first is an infraction, regardless of whether anything happens.
- The Academy's relationship with House Mimolot's tariff system means that some knowledge is formally restricted to licensed recipients. Requesting restricted knowledge without establishing license is a query that generates a report, not a denial.
- Scholarly decorum in the halls requires that ongoing academic arguments be pursued in writing, not aloud in shared spaces. Public disputes between faculty are considered indecorous regardless of merit.

## Signature Details

- **The ward-line ground paths.** The paths between Academy buildings are set with faint ward lines at regular intervals — visible as slightly different-colored stone. Most are passive. Some are active in ways the institution declines to specify. Students learn to register which ones hum faintly under their feet, as a practical exercise in sensitivity.
- **The verification clerk's forms.** Every visitor to the restricted archive completes a form that captures purpose, affiliation, and the specific materials they intend to access. The form is a legal document under House Mimolot's knowledge tariff framework. Signing it with false information is not merely a social breach but a tariff violation.
- **The faculty monograph board.** A large pinboard in the faculty common hall tracks work-in-progress: titles, draft stages, expected completion. A draft that has been on the board for more than a year without advancing is a visible record of difficulty, and faculty learn to read the board for political as well as academic information.

## Complication Seed

A third-year student has submitted a formal request to access a restricted theoretical framework in the archive — a body of work tariff-locked under House Mimolot's knowledge licensing system. The request was denied on procedural grounds: the student's current program classification does not include the relevant knowledge tier. The student believes the denial is not procedural but political, because the framework she wants to access was developed by a scholar who left the Academy under disputed circumstances five years ago. The archive clerk who processes the requests is not in a position to speak to institutional politics — but she has processed enough requests to know which ones are about scholarship and which ones are about something the institution would rather not revisit.
