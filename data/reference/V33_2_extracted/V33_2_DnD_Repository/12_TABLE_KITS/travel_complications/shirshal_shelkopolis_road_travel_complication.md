# Shirshal - Shelkopolis road Travel Complications

**Core route identity:** Post-industrial road through Roaz polity extraction terrain, with two Roaz company toll stations and ore processing convoys that dominate right-of-way.

**Checkpoint problem:** Travel along Shirshal - Shelkopolis road is easiest when intentions, cargo, and patronage are legible before questions begin. Now make one of those suddenly unclear.

**Patrol complication:** Daylight oversight is visible and procedural; night oversight is thinner, more suspicious, and less forgiving. The party arrives at the wrong patrol moment.

**Night complication:** After sunset, traffic thins, trust narrows, and informal power brokers matter more than posted rules. Someone offers a shortcut with a future cost.

**Seasonal pressure:** ore convoy scheduling delays, toll station inspection backlog, dust and grit reducing road visibility in dry months

**Hazard twist:** Roaz extraction company toll operators hold de facto authority over passage; Roadwarden jurisdiction is nominal here, and bandits targeting wage laborers know the Wardens arrive late.
