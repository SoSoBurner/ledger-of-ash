# Guildheart Hub - Shelkopolis road Travel Complications

**Core route identity:** Well-maintained trade road between two commercial centers, actively policed by guild factor checkpoints with cargo inspection authority and occasional guild courier escorts available for hire.

**Checkpoint problem:** Travel along Guildheart Hub - Shelkopolis road is easiest when intentions, cargo, and patronage are legible before questions begin. Now make one of those suddenly unclear.

**Patrol complication:** Daylight oversight is visible and procedural; night oversight is thinner, more suspicious, and less forgiving. The party arrives at the wrong patrol moment.

**Night complication:** After sunset, traffic thins, trust narrows, and informal power brokers matter more than posted rules. Someone offers a shortcut with a future cost.

**Seasonal pressure:** trade fair surges, guild courier schedule conflicts, inspection backlog during peak commerce seasons

**Hazard twist:** The road is safe but slow — bureaucratic friction at factor checkpoints is constant, and legitimate cargo inspections can strand travelers for hours while guild ledgers are reconciled.
