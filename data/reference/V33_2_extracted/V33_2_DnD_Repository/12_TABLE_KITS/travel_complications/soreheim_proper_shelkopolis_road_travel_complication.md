# Soreheim Proper - Shelkopolis road Travel Complications

**Core route identity:** Industrial freight road under Shelkopolis economic pressure, dominated by ore cart traffic with Shelk trade officer checkpoints verifying manifests and worker hostility toward Shelk-affiliated travelers.

**Checkpoint problem:** Travel along Soreheim Proper - Shelkopolis road is easiest when intentions, cargo, and patronage are legible before questions begin. Now make one of those suddenly unclear.

**Patrol complication:** Daylight oversight is visible and procedural; night oversight is thinner, more suspicious, and less forgiving. The party arrives at the wrong patrol moment.

**Night complication:** After sunset, traffic thins, trust narrows, and informal power brokers matter more than posted rules. Someone offers a shortcut with a future cost.

**Seasonal pressure:** ore cart scheduling surges, manifest inspection backlog at Shelk trade posts, road damage from heavy freight loads

**Hazard twist:** Shelk trade officers hold checkpoint authority but Soreheim industrial supervisors hold local knowledge — a traveler who satisfies the trade post manifests may still face obstruction from workers who read Shelk affiliation into cargo, dress, or documentation style.
