# Panim Haven - Shelkopolis road Travel Complications

**Core route identity:** Mixed road and coastal route with tidal timing constraints, fishing village stopovers, and coastal Roadwarden vessel inspections of passing traffic.

**Checkpoint problem:** Travel along Panim Haven - Shelkopolis road is easiest when intentions, cargo, and patronage are legible before questions begin. Now make one of those suddenly unclear.

**Patrol complication:** Daylight oversight is visible and procedural; night oversight is thinner, more suspicious, and less forgiving. The party arrives at the wrong patrol moment.

**Night complication:** After sunset, traffic thins, trust narrows, and informal power brokers matter more than posted rules. Someone offers a shortcut with a future cost.

**Seasonal pressure:** tidal window closures on the coastal segment, storm weather disrupting fishing village stopovers, supply interruption during low-tide periods

**Hazard twist:** The coastal segment's tidal timing is non-negotiable — miss the window and the route adds a full day's delay, during which fishing village locals offer shelter that comes with obligations and smuggler awareness that attracts scrutiny.
