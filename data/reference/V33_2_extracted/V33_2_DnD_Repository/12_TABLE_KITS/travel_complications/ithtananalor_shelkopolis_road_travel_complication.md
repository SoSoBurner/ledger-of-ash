# Ithtananalor - Shelkopolis road Travel Complications

**Core route identity:** Highland border crossing beneath the Sheresh Dome's shadow into Shelkopolis Principalities territory, with Roadwarden checkpoint at the final approach and Sheresh Dome Steward patrols near the border line.

**Checkpoint problem:** Travel along Ithtananalor - Shelkopolis road is easiest when intentions, cargo, and patronage are legible before questions begin. Now make one of those suddenly unclear.

**Patrol complication:** Daylight oversight is visible and procedural; night oversight is thinner, more suspicious, and less forgiving. The party arrives at the wrong patrol moment.

**Night complication:** After sunset, traffic thins, trust narrows, and informal power brokers matter more than posted rules. Someone offers a shortcut with a future cost.

**Seasonal pressure:** highland weather delays, border inspection backlog, elevation-based road closures after heavy snowfall

**Hazard twist:** Jurisdictional friction between Sheresh Dome Stewards and Shelkopolis Roadwardens creates competing checkpoint demands — traveler documents satisfy one authority and alarm the other.
