# Sunspire Haven - Shelkopolis road Travel Complications

**Core route identity:** Pilgrimage road to a sacred site, with shrine maintenance workers, pilgrimage caravans slowing throughput, and Roadwarden checkpoints at the sacred site perimeter.

**Checkpoint problem:** Travel along Sunspire Haven - Shelkopolis road is easiest when intentions, cargo, and patronage are legible before questions begin. Now make one of those suddenly unclear.

**Patrol complication:** Daylight oversight is visible and procedural; night oversight is thinner, more suspicious, and less forgiving. The party arrives at the wrong patrol moment.

**Night complication:** After sunset, traffic thins, trust narrows, and informal power brokers matter more than posted rules. Someone offers a shortcut with a future cost.

**Seasonal pressure:** pilgrimage season surges, border friction between Roadwardens and Sheresh stewards, shrine perimeter closures during rites

**Hazard twist:** Territorial friction between Shelkopolis Roadwardens and Sheresh stewards at the border creates split checkpoint authority — pilgrims who offer shelter are hospitality-bound allies but their visible presence marks travelers as shrine-adjacent, attracting additional scrutiny at both checkpoints.
